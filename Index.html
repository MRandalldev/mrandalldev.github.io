FILE 001

Who I Am

Hi, my name is Mekhi Randall. I am a Computer Engineering and Computer Science student at North Carolina Agricultural and Technical State University with a passion for creating innovative solutions.

I was born in Trenton, New Jersey and raised in Morrisville, Pennsylvania. I have been interested in engineering since I was a kid. I loved taking things apart, figuring out how they worked, and putting them back together.

At the same time, I grew up loving video games and wanted to understand how they were made. That curiosity led me into coding classes and eventually programming, giving me an interest in both the hardware and software sides of computing.

FILE 002

What I'm Working Toward

I am working toward a career in computer engineering and software development, with a particular interest in hardware, embedded systems, computer systems, and the connection between physical devices and the software that controls them.

I also enjoy building software and learning new technologies. My goal is to keep developing both sides of my skill set so I can work on projects that combine engineering, programming, troubleshooting, and creative problem solving.

PROJECT 001 / 2024

Kai's PCs — PC Refurbishment & Custom Builds

In 2024, I started Kai's PCs, where I sourced used computer components and rebuilt them into complete desktop systems for resale on eBay.

The work gave me hands-on experience identifying components, checking compatibility, assembling systems, troubleshooting hardware problems, testing completed builds, and preparing finished PCs for customers.

These are three systems I assembled while operating Kai's PCs.

PROJECT 002 / 2025

Kai's Electronics — Custom PSP Refurbishment

In 2025, as PC component prices continued rising, I expanded the business from Kai's PCs into Kai's Electronics and began focusing more broadly on refurbishing and reselling consumer electronics and game consoles.

My first console project was a custom PSP. I worked through the refurbishment process, restored the system, customized its appearance, and prepared it for resale.
